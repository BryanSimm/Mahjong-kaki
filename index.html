Singapore-style mahjong for four. Play three bots on your own, or open a table and send your kakis the code.

Rules used at this table

148 tiles: dots, bamboo and characters (1–9, four of each), four winds, three dragons, eight flowers and seasons, and four animals (cat, mouse, rooster, centipede). Bonus tiles go face-up and you draw a replacement.

Win with four sets (pong, kong or chow) plus a pair, or thirteen wonders. To win off someone's discard you need at least 1 tai. A hand with no tai can only win by self-draw (自摸), and then counts as 1 tai. Scoring caps at 5 tai. Chow only from the player on your left; Hu beats Pong/Kong, which beats Chow.

Payout unit doubles per tai: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16. Win off a discard: the shooter pays double, the others pay single. Self-drawn: everyone pays double. Instant "bites": cat + mouse or rooster + centipede, everyone pays 1; exposed kong, everyone pays 1; concealed kong, everyone pays 2.

Everyone starts at 0 points. The game ends after four wind rounds, or when the game time limit (if you set one) runs out. The dealer keeps the deal after winning or a drawn hand.
