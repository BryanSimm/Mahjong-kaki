Rules used at this table

148 tiles: dots, bamboo and characters (1–9, four of each), four winds, three dragons, eight flowers and seasons, and four animals (cat, mouse, rooster, centipede). Bonus tiles go face-up and you draw a replacement.

Win with four sets (pong, kong or chow) plus a pair, or thirteen wonders. Winning by self-draw (自摸) adds 1 tai. A hand with no tai can still win and counts as 1 tai (off a discard: the shooter pays 2, the others pay 1), or 2 tai if self-drawn. One exception: a no-tai hand waiting in the middle (like 5 _ 7 waiting on 6) can only win by self-draw (自摸). Scoring caps at 5 tai. The yellow 台 chip next to each name shows the tai already on the table: flowers or seasons matching that seat, every animal, and exposed pongs of dragons or the seat/round wind (hover or long-press to see why). Chow only from the player on your left; Hu beats Pong/Kong, which beats Chow.

Ping hu pays its own amount: off a discard the shooter pays 10 and the others pay 5; self-drawn, all three pay 12.

Off a discard, the shooter pays the bigger amount and the other two pay the smaller one. Self-drawn (自摸) adds 1 tai (not for ping hu), then all three pay the shooter amount. Instant "bites": cat + mouse or rooster + centipede, everyone pays 1; exposed kong, everyone pays 1; concealed kong, everyone pays 2.

Everyone starts at 0 points. The game ends after four wind rounds, or when the game time limit (if you set one) runs out. The dealer keeps the deal after winning or a drawn hand.
